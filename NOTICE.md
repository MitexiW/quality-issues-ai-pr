Hao Li, Haoxiang Zhang, and Ahmed E. Hassan. *AIDev: Studying AI Coding
Agents on GitHub*. Zenodo, 2025. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16919051

## Public GitHub repositories

The artifact records public repository identifiers, pull-request numbers,
commit identifiers, paths, and derived measurements needed for research
auditability. It does not redistribute cloned repository source trees.
Third-party repository content remains subject to the license and terms of its
originating repository; the licenses in this artifact do not supersede those
