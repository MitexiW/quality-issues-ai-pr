# Analysis Deviations Log

Current amended specification:
`study_stars500-introduced-quality-first-full-sample-no-time-2026-08-14`

This append-only log records every change to outcomes, eligibility, comparability
design, covariates, models, multiplicity, sensitivity analyses, and reporting rules
after the RQ2 specification was frozen. A row is required even when a deviation is
caused by missing data, model non-convergence, or inadequate common support.

The specification was frozen while the Human CodeQL experiment was incomplete but
after partial Human pipeline results already existed. It is therefore a
pre-final-results protocol, not a fully prospective preregistration.

## Required entry fields

Each entry must contain:

- sequential deviation ID;
- UTC decision timestamp;
- affected specification path;
- original rule;
- replacement rule or operational decision;
- reason known before inspecting the affected final outcome, where applicable;
- whether the decision was made before or after inspecting final group effects;
- expected influence on the estimand or interpretation;
- files, commands, and people responsible for the decision;
- analyses that retain the original rule as a sensitivity comparison.
